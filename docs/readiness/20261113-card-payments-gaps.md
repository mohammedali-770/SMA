# Card payments: product, code and config gaps (verified)

> **Status: DRAFT, engineering-written 2026-11-13, for the owner.** Nothing was
> changed to produce it. Every check was a read of the default branch at `04eb351` or a
> read-only query on Production (project `wxfmmnihidsdyemasstf`). No secret was
> read or printed. Fixing any item below is its own piece of work, and anything
> that writes to Production, deploys or builds is a CLAUDE.md §5 approval.

**What this is.** The fact-checks of the five card-payment drafts (privacy policy,
payment policy, cancellation & refund policy, store answers, proxy comparison)
found problems that better wording cannot fix. Each one below was re-checked
against the code and the live database, with an honest attempt to disprove it.
29 survived. The fact-checks are in `docs/readiness/20261113-card-payments-reviews/`
(review IDs such as "payment review B2" refer to them); the drafts are in
`docs/legal/`, `docs/store/` and `docs/integrations/`.

**How to read the evidence.** Paths are relative to the repository root, at the
default branch `04eb351`. "Live" means a read-only query on Production on 2026-11-13.
The state at that moment: online payment **off**; the Moyasar row **enabled, TEST
mode**, invoice expiry 30 minutes; the refund job **inactive**; `order_refunds`
empty; **0** Moyasar payment records; 82 orders, all from 7 accounts (team
accounts, per the proxy draft).

**The short version.**

- **Must be fixed before §47.18 (11):** PG-01 to PG-11. Seven are code or
  migration work (refunds nobody sees, double charges nobody is told about, a
  cancelled order that can still be sent to the kitchen, the cash lock-out, the
  "use fewer points" message, account deletion wiping card records, the POS not
  reliably told an order is paid). Four are documents and settings.
- **Need your decision before §47.18 (8):** PG-12 to PG-17, PG-19, PG-20.
- **Real, but not card blockers (10):** PG-18, PG-21 to PG-29. Three of them are
  **already true in Production today, card or no card**: order status never moves
  past "Received" (PG-21), loyalty points are never credited (PG-22), and
  self-service account deletion stalls for anyone who has ordered (PG-23).
- Items marked **(new)** were found while verifying and are not in any review.
