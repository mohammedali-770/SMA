Using the attached design system, design the checkout and payment screens for the Spicy Meal customer app. Phone artboards at 390x844, in a row with a title label above each.

1. Checkout, ARABIC right-to-left, the complete state. Cards in order: order type (نوع الطلب) with a delivery chip; delivery location (موقع التوصيل) showing "المنزل · بجانب بقالة السلام" and a change chip; payment method (طريقة الدفع) as two segmented options, "الدفع الإلكتروني" selected and "نقداً عند التوصيل" unselected; a promo code field with an apply button and a green "تم تطبيق الخصم!" confirmation; a loyalty redemption row with a toggle switched on and "240 نقطة متاحة"; a kitchen notes field. Then a totals card with rows: المجموع الفرعي 78.00, رسوم التوصيل 10.00, خصم الحملة (20٪) −15.60 in green, خصم الولاء −5.00 in green, ضريبة القيمة المضافة (١٥٪ شاملة) 8.79 in muted grey, and a bold الإجمالي 67.40 ر.س. Sticky confirm button "تأكيد وإرسال الطلب".
2. Checkout, BLOCKED state, ARABIC. Same screen but with an amber warning notice at the top reading that the order is below the branch delivery minimum and needs 7.00 ر.س more; a red inline notice in the payment card saying online payment is unavailable and cash is available; and a sticky footer where the confirm button is DISABLED grey with a single blocking reason stated above it.
3. Secure payment, ARABIC. A screen titled "الدفع الآمن" with a close action, a green encrypted-connection notice, and the payment provider's hosted card form rendered inside — total amount displayed large, card number, expiry and CVV fields, and a pay button. A footnote that the page is hosted by the provider inside the app.
4. Payment verification, ARABIC. A centred screen with a circular spinner icon, the heading "جارٍ التحقق من الدفع…", explanatory text not to close the app, a thin progress bar, and a note that the server confirms the payment, not the page.
5. Receipt in the CONFIRMED state, ARABIC. A card with a green circular checkmark, "تم تأكيد الطلب", the line "قام الفرع بتأكيد طلبك.", then a divider and the label "رقم الطلب لدى الفرع" above a very large "#5". Below: a details card with payment (مدفوع, green chip), branch and timestamp; then an order summary card with line items, delivery fee, discounts in green and a bold total. Two sticky buttons: "طلباتي" outlined and "القائمة" filled.
6. My orders, ARABIC. A list of three order cards, each with a status pill (قيد التحضير amber, جارٍ التحقق مع الفرع neutral, تم التوصيل green), a timestamp, the branch and order type, the total price, and a line showing the branch order number or "لم يصدر بعد". Bottom tab bar with the orders tab active.

Critical rule: the internal database order number must never appear. Only the branch's own POS number is ever shown to a customer, and only once it exists.

IMAGE RULE — REQUIRED FOR THE FILE TO SAVE. Do NOT insert image assets, image fills, or any external/remote image reference anywhere in this design. Every photograph must be drawn as a rounded rectangle filled with a warm appetising linear gradient (for food: #ffd9a3 into #f4a259 into #d1512f; vary the hue slightly between dishes) and, where it helps, a small muted caption. These are photo placeholders, which is standard mockup practice — production builds swap in real product shots at the same crop. Maps are likewise drawn with vector strokes, never an image tile.
