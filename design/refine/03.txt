CRAFT PASS — make these screens feel designed by a person, not generated.

The current screens are correct but generic: every card is the same height, the
brand purple is sprayed everywhere, the type sits at one weight, and the food
blocks read as flat swatches. Fix the CRAFT, not the structure. Do not add,
remove or rename any screen, control, field, button, tab, badge or feature. Do
not invent menu items, promotions, ratings, delivery times or any content that is
not already on the screen.

Apply all of the following:

1. TYPE CONTRAST. Widen the gap between levels. Product and screen names go
   heavier and tighter (negative letter-spacing); descriptions go lighter, smaller
   and a step cooler in colour. Prices are the second-loudest thing on a card
   after the name. Right now everything sits at a similar weight and it reads flat.

2. SPACING RHYTHM. Stop spacing everything evenly. Give section starts generous
   breathing room above them and tighten the gaps inside a group, so the eye
   groups content instead of scanning a uniform ladder. Vary the vertical rhythm
   between sections rather than repeating one gap value.

3. DEPTH. Cards should sit on the background, not be outlined on it. Prefer a
   soft, low, wide shadow over a hairline border. Let the food block bleed to the
   card's edge on at least the first card of a list instead of floating inset with
   equal padding on all four sides.

4. FOOD BLOCKS. These stand in for photography, so make them read as a photo crop,
   not a colour chip: layered multi-stop gradients on an off-axis angle, a warm
   highlight offset from centre, a darker vignette at one corner, and a slightly
   different hue and angle per dish. No two dishes identical.

5. BRAND RESTRAINT. Purple is currently everywhere. Pull it back to primary
   actions, the active navigation state and one accent per screen. Let surfaces be
   warm near-white and let the food carry the colour. The red stays reserved for
   destructive and error states only.

6. OPTICAL DETAIL. Align Arabic text optically rather than mechanically, keep
   corner radii consistent within a screen, and let numerals sit on a tabular
   baseline so columns of prices line up.

Keep every existing label, string, state, warning and control exactly as it is.
This is a visual craft pass only.
